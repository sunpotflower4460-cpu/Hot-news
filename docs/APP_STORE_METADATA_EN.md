## App name

Bright News | Hot News

## Subtitle

Three genuinely bright stories a day

## Promotional text

Take a breath and reconnect with the world through a small number of verified, genuinely bright stories. Start with a 30-second summary, check the source, and stop whenever you have had enough.

## Description

Hot News is a calm news app that selects only events whose core is genuinely bright, hopeful, or joyful.

It does not soften tragic news or disguise difficult events with positive wording. Every published story must pass editorial checks for brightness, emotional safety, reliability, source quality, rights, and reader burden.

Key features:

- three selected bright stories each day
- a 30-second summary before the full article
- clear source links and verification history
- Japanese and English language controls
- saved and recently read stories kept on your device
- purpose-first discovery, including quick reads, kindness, future-focused stories, family conversation, and bedtime reading
- calm light, dark, morning, day, evening, and night themes
- no streaks, missed-day warnings, unread pressure, or infinite-feed requirement
- privacy and diagnostics denied by default

The initial production version must describe only features that are actually enabled in the submitted binary.
